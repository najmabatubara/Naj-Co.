Founder of Batik Betawi Terogong in South Jakarta. Motivated by the decline of Kampung Terogong's batik tradition, she revived the craft by preserving authentic Betawi motifs and empowering local women through batik making.

Cultural Story

Batik Ondel-ondel Burung Hong

The cultural icons in this batik represent spiritual symbols and centers of cultural acculturation, preserving the values and identity of the Betawi people through every motif.

Ondel-Ondel

Once believed to represent ancestral guardians, Ondel-Ondel stands as the most recognizable symbol of Betawi identity, representing resilience, protection, and community spirit.

Burung Hong

Inspired by the Chinese phoenix, Burung Hong reflects centuries of cultural exchange that shaped Betawi heritage, symbolizing prosperity, harmony, and elegance.

Every illustration represents a piece of Jakarta's cultural identity—from traditional houses, performing arts, to daily culinary heritage—reinterpreted in contemporary fashion.

Sustainability Impact

This collection is designed to create impact beyond the product itself. Organic linen replaces synthetic fabrics, while post-consumer denim is carefully upcycled into new design elements.

Crafted in collaboration with Betawi batik artisans and local sewing partners, supporting traditional craftsmanship while strengthening community-based production.

Care & Longevity

Gentle Washing

Hand wash or machine wash on a delicate cycle using cold water and mild detergent.

Air Dry Naturally

Dry in the shade and avoid direct sunlight to maintain fabric color and strength.

Iron with Care

Iron at a low to medium temperature with a pressing cloth over batik panels.
